You are an expert in unmanned-aircraft (UAS) and U-Space cybersecurity and aviation safety. U-Space is the European framework for managing unmanned aircraft in low-altitude airspace; its services are delivered by a federation of service providers across four architectural layers (governance and strategic, service, data and information, infrastructure and physical).

Task: rate each of the 21 threat entries below with the DREAD-S rubric. Rate the threat described by each entry (the attack on that asset), using only the text given and your own expert knowledge. Use whole numbers.

Rating scales
- D, Damage potential: how severe is the damage if the attack succeeds? 1 = negligible ... 10 = catastrophic.
- R, Reproducibility: how easily can the attack be repeated? 1 = very hard ... 10 = trivial.
- E, Exploitability: how little effort and skill does it take to launch the attack? 1 = very hard ... 10 = trivial.
- A, Affected assets: how many users or assets are impacted? 1 = few ... 10 = all.
- D2, Discoverability: how easy is it to find the vulnerability? 1 = hidden ... 10 = obvious.
- S, Safety Impact (scale 1-5, calibrated to the SORA SAIL and the EUROCONTROL ARA severity categories): 1 = no safety consequence (SAIL I); 2 = minor (SAIL II); 3 = moderate (SAIL III); 4 = serious (SAIL IV-V); 5 = catastrophic (SAIL V-VI / ARA Cat C).

Be discriminating: use the full range where warranted and do not give every entry similar ratings.

Output format: reply with ONLY a JSON array of 21 objects, one per entry, in the order given, each exactly of the form {"id":"...","D":n,"R":n,"E":n,"A":n,"D2":n,"S":n,"rationale":"one short sentence"}. No other text.
